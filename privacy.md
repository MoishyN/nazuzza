# Privacy Policy — Nazuza

*Last updated: August 2026*

## Overview

Nazuza is a public transit planning app for Israel. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect

**Information you provide:**
- If you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and an Apple-generated email address solely to personalize your experience within the app.
- Favorite places (Home and Work addresses) you save in the app.

**Information stored locally on your device:**
- Your recent destination searches (last 3)
- Your saved favorite places
- Your transport mode preferences
- Whether you have scanned a RavKav card

None of this information is transmitted to our servers. It lives only on your device.

**Location:**
- We access your location while using the app to show nearby stops and plan routes from your current position.
- We do not store, transmit, or share your location history.

## Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect trip history
- We do not track you across other apps or websites
- We do not sell your data to anyone
- We do not show advertising

## Transit Information

All public transportation information displayed in Nazuza — including routes, schedules, stop locations, and real-time data — is sourced from the Israeli Ministry of Transport (MOT) and the National Transport Authority (NTA). Nazuza is not responsible for the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of this information.

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

We may use anonymous crash reporting and analytics tools in the future to improve the app. If and when we do, this policy will be updated.

## Data Security

Your preferences and favorites are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure SwiftData framework. We do not operate user accounts or store personal data on our servers.

## Children's Privacy

Nazuza is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of Nazuza after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
